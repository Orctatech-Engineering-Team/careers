# Hiring Process

**Updated: May 2026**

Our hiring process is designed to be transparent, fast, and humane. From application to offer within 7 days.

## Philosophy

- **No ghosting** — every applicant gets a reply within 24 hours
- **No LeetCode** — we evaluate what you build, not what you memorized
- **No take-home assignments** — our practical session is 90 minutes, collaborative, and on a real Orcta problem
- **No exploding offers** — we want you to feel good about saying yes

## Process

### Day 1: Apply (5 min)

A short form. No cover letter needed. Ask them to link something they have built.

**Review filter:** Do they have solid fundamentals? Do they care about craft? Past experience matters less than how they think about their work.

### Days 1-2: Human review (24 hrs)

A real person reads every application. Everyone gets a reply within 24 hours.

### Days 2-3: Casual chat (30 min)

No whiteboarding. No algorithm puzzles. A conversation about what they build and what we are working on.

**Look for:** Do they think clearly about what they have built? Are they curious about our problems? Do they communicate well?

### Days 3-5: Practical session (90 min)

Collaborative problem-solving on a real Orcta problem. The candidate works with one of us on a task that resembles actual work.

**Look for:** How do they approach unfamiliar problems? Do they ask good questions? Can they work through ambiguity?

### Days 5-7: Offer

Decision within 48 hours of the practical session. No exploding offers. We share salary range and let them take the time they need.

## Role-Specific Considerations

| Role | Key Signal |
|------|-----------|
| Frontend Lead | Component architecture thinking, UI sensibility |
| Backend Lead | Schema design, data integrity mindset |
| Systems & Fullstack | Comfort across the stack, infrastructure awareness |
